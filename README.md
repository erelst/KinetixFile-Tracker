# KinetixFile Tracker

> Bug reports, feature requests, and project updates for [KinetixFile](https://kinetixfile.com).

## About This Repository

KinetixFile is a free, private, in-browser file converter built independently with everyday hardware and a modest budget. Everything runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no accounts, no nonsense.

Because we are small and budget-conscious, we may introduce non-intrusive ads in the future to keep the project running. We are committed to keeping ads minimal and unobtrusive — your experience always comes first.

- Or support directly at [donatr.ee/erelst](https://donatr.ee/erelst/)

## Bug Reports & Feature Requests

We welcome bug reports and feature requests. Here's what to know:

- **All reports are read** — but not every report will receive a reply or be accepted.
- **Priority matters** — we focus on critical issues that affect the most users. Minor tweaks and niche requests may not be addressed.
- **Write clearly** — detailed, well-described reports get faster attention.

### Tips for Writing Better Reports

If you're not technical, we recommend **chatting with an AI assistant first** to help structure your report. A clear description helps us understand and act on your issue faster.

A good report includes:

- What happened (expected vs actual behavior)
- Steps to reproduce
- Browser and device info
- Screenshots if applicable
